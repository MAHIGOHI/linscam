Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

LinsCam AI ("LinsCam AI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is designed to help you understand images using artificial intelligence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the LinsCam AI mobile application ("App"), how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By using LinsCam AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. WHAT LINSCAM AI DOES

LinsCam AI allows users to upload or select an image and request AI-powered analysis, explanations, checks, or other image-related assistance.

The App may process the image you choose in order to provide the AI response requested by you.

LinsCam AI does not require an account, username, password, phone number, contacts, or social-media account to use its core image-analysis functionality.

2. IMAGES YOU CHOOSE TO ANALYZE

When you select an image for analysis, the selected image may be transmitted to our backend and/or an AI service provider used to generate the requested response.

The image is processed to provide the functionality requested by you.

Images uploaded by users may potentially contain personal or sensitive information, even when the user does not intentionally include such information.

For your protection, do not upload images containing information you do not want processed by an AI service or transmitted over the Internet. This may include:

  • Government identification documents
  • Bank or payment information
  • Passwords or authentication codes
  • Private conversations
  • Medical records
  • Personal addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Facial images of other people
  • Children's personal information
  • Any other confidential or sensitive information

3. USER CONFIRMATION BEFORE IMAGE PROCESSING

Before submitting an image for AI analysis, LinsCam AI may ask the user to confirm that they understand the image they are submitting should not contain personal or sensitive information unless they intentionally choose to provide it.

This confirmation is intended to provide transparency and user awareness.

This confirmation does not mean that an uploaded image can never contain personal information. If an image accidentally contains personal information, it may still be processed as part of providing the requested functionality.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PROCESSING

LinsCam AI uses artificial intelligence services to analyze images and generate responses.

Depending on the feature and technical implementation, image data and relevant request information may be transmitted to a third-party AI service provider, including Google or another provider used by LinsCam AI.

The AI service may be used to:

  • Understand the contents of an image
  • Extract relevant visual context
  • Explain information visible in an image
  • Identify potential issues or warnings
  • Provide AI-generated responses requested by the user

AI-generated responses may contain mistakes, incomplete information, or inaccurate interpretations. LinsCam AI does not guarantee that an AI-generated response is always accurate.

AI responses should not be treated as professional medical, legal, financial, security, or other professional advice.

5. AI SERVICE PROVIDERS

To provide AI-powered functionality, LinsCam AI may use third-party artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Where such services are used, submitted data may be processed by the applicable service provider according to its applicable terms, privacy practices, and technical policies.

We do not sell uploaded images to third parties.

We do not use uploaded images for advertising purposes.

6. ADVERTISING AND ADMOB

LinsCam AI may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

AdMob may process certain information associated with advertising and ad delivery, subject to Google's applicable policies and settings. Advertising-related information may include, where applicable:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location or other information used for advertising and measurement
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

The exact information processed can depend on the user's device, region, consent choices, age treatment, and applicable advertising configuration.

LinsCam AI does not sell users' uploaded images to advertisers. Ads are separate from the AI image-analysis functionality.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

LinsCam AI takes children's privacy seriously. The App is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

LinsCam AI does not require children to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, or other identifying information to use the core functionality.

However, users may upload arbitrary images, and an image may contain information relating to a child. Users should not upload:

  • Children's identity documents
  • Children's medical records
  • Children's private conversations
  • School records containing identifying information
  • Images containing sensitive information about a child
  • Any other confidential information relating to a child

If LinsCam AI is configured or distributed as an app targeting children or as an app whose target audience includes children, additional Google Play Families requirements may apply.

8. ADVERTISING FOR CHILDREN AND USERS OF UNKNOWN AGE

Where applicable, LinsCam AI will use the appropriate age-treatment and advertising configuration required by Google Play and AdMob.

For users subject to child-directed treatment, appropriate advertising safeguards will be applied according to applicable Google policies and configuration requirements.

9. INFORMATION WE DO NOT INTENTIONALLY COLLECT

LinsCam AI does not require users to provide the following information for its core functionality:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Password
  • Social-media account
  • Government identification
  • Payment-card information
  • Precise location

However, information visible inside an image may be processed if the user chooses to upload that image.

10. APP HISTORY

LinsCam AI may provide a history feature so users can revisit previous analyses.

Where history is stored locally on the user's device, that information is controlled by the device and the App's local storage.

Deleting an item from the App's history may remove the locally stored copy.

11. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through LinsCam AI.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting confidential or highly sensitive information through the App.

12. DATA RETENTION

LinsCam AI does not intentionally retain user-submitted images longer than reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, subject to the retention practices of third-party service providers and the technical architecture used to process the request.

Where applicable, data may be temporarily processed or retained by infrastructure or AI service providers according to their respective policies.

13. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

LinsCam AI may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google AI services and/or other AI infrastructure for AI-powered image analysis
  • Google Play services and Android platform services for app functionality

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

LinsCam AI does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers.

14. PERMISSIONS

LinsCam AI requests only permissions that are necessary for the functionality implemented in the App.

Depending on the Android version and implementation, image selection may use Android's system image-selection mechanism.

The App does not require access to contacts, microphone, phone calls, or precise location for its core image-analysis functionality.

15. NO SALE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

LinsCam AI does not sell users' uploaded images or personal information to advertisers or other third parties.

We may use third-party service providers when necessary to operate the App and provide requested functionality. Such processing is different from selling personal information.

16. USER RESPONSIBILITY

Users are responsible for the content they choose to upload.

You should not upload an image unless you have the right to provide that image for processing.

You should also avoid uploading confidential information unless you understand the consequences of transmitting that information to the services required to process your request.

17. ACCURACY OF AI RESPONSES

AI-generated responses are provided for informational purposes.

LinsCam AI may incorrectly interpret:

  • Text and Documents
  • Screenshots
  • Medical or Financial information
  • Messages
  • Objects and People
  • Visual context

Users should independently verify important information. LinsCam AI should not be relied upon as a substitute for qualified professional advice.

18. AI SAFETY

LinsCam AI is designed to provide useful image understanding while taking reasonable measures to reduce harmful or inappropriate outputs.

AI systems can occasionally produce incorrect, incomplete, or inappropriate responses.

Users should report problematic AI responses through the available reporting or feedback mechanism where provided.

19. USER CHOICES

You may:

  • Choose whether to upload an image
  • Cancel image selection
  • Delete locally stored analysis history where the feature permits
  • Stop using the App
  • Avoid submitting sensitive information
  • Control applicable Android permissions through device settings
  • Manage applicable advertising and privacy settings provided by Android, Google, or your region

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.
