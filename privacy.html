Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Scan & Explain (formerly LinsCam AI), developed by LINS IO ("we", "us", or "our"), respects your privacy and is designed to help you understand images, medicines, messages, and documents using artificial intelligence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Scan & Explain mobile application ("App"), how images and advertising data are handled, and the choices available to you.

By using Scan & Explain, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. WHAT SCAN & EXPLAIN DOES

Scan & Explain allows users to upload or select an image and request AI-powered explanations, including medicine information checks, scam or suspicious message checks, document summaries, and general image explanations.

The App does not require an account, username, password, phone number, contacts, or social-media login to use its core functionality.

2. IMAGES YOU CHOOSE TO ANALYZE

When you select an image and tap Analyze, the selected image (compressed on your device) along with any optional text context and selected response language is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend server (Cloudflare Workers) and forwarded to a third-party AI service provider to generate your requested explanation.

We do not permanently store your uploaded images on our backend servers. Images are processed transiently solely to return the requested analysis.

Because images may accidentally contain personal or confidential details, please do not upload images containing:

  • Government identification documents (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driver's license)
  • Banking, UPI, credit/debit card, or payment details
  • Passwords, OTPs, or authentication codes
  • Private personal conversations
  • Confidential medical records containing personal identifiers
  • Personal home addresses or phone numbers
  • Facial images of other individuals without their consent
  • Children's personal or identifying information

3. USER CONFIRMATION BEFORE PROCESSING

Before submitting an image for AI analysis, Scan & Explain asks you to confirm that the image does not contain sensitive personal, financial, or banking information.

This confirmation is provided for transparency and user safety. If an uploaded image accidentally includes personal information, it may still be processed by the AI service as part of fulfilling your request.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PROCESSING & PROVIDERS

To analyze images and generate structured responses, Scan & Explain transmits image data and request context through our Cloudflare Workers backend to third-party AI service providers (such as Google AI / Gemini or equivalent AI infrastructure).

The AI service processes your image to:

  • Understand and summarize visible content
  • Explain medicine packaging, documents, or general images
  • Highlight potential scam indicators or key details
  • Generate structured responses in your chosen language (English or Hindi)

Data transmitted to third-party AI providers is handled under their respective privacy policies and terms. We do not sell uploaded images to third parties, and we do not use your uploaded images for advertising purposes.

5. ADVERTISING AND GOOGLE ADMOB

Scan & Explain displays advertisements—including interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads (to unlock additional scans)—using Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

  • Advertising ID (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language)
  • IP address and approximate location (for regional ad delivery and fraud prevention)
  • App usage, ad views, and ad interaction data
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and performance metrics

The exact data processed depends on your device settings, region, consent preferences, and Google's privacy policies. You can learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps at Google Privacy & Terms.

Ads are completely separate from image analysis. We never share your uploaded images with advertisers.

6. LOCAL STORAGE & SCAN HISTORY

Scan & Explain stores certain data locally on your device using local app storage:

  • Your recent analysis history (text results only) so you can revisit past explanations
  • App preferences (dark/light theme, app language, and AI response language)
  • Daily free scan counter and rewarded scan balance
  • Your legal consent status

This data remains on your device. You can delete individual history items or clear all history anytime from the App's Settings, or remove all local data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

7. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

Scan & Explain does not ask for or require:

  • Full name, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS inbox access
  • Microphone or precise GPS location
  • User accounts or passwords
  • Payment or card details

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Scan & Explain is not directed toward children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users must not upload images containing children's identity documents, medical records, school records, or other identifying information. If you believe personal information about a child has been inadvertently submitted, please contact us so we can assist where possible.

9. ACCURACY & DISCLAIMER (MEDICAL, SCAM & DOCUMENT)

All AI-generated explanations—including Medicine Check, Scam Check, Document Summary, and General Scan—are provided strictly for informational purposes only.

AI can make mistakes and may misread text, packaging, numbers, or context. Responses must not be treated as professional medical, healthcare, legal, financial, or cybersecurity advice. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before taking any medication, and verify suspicious links or financial requests through official channels.

10. DATA SECURITY & RETENTION

All image transmissions between the App, our backend (Cloudflare Workers), and AI service providers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS. We do not retain user-uploaded images on our backend after the analysis request completes. Temporary processing or standard operational logs by infrastructure or AI providers are governed by their respective retention policies.

No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure; therefore, please avoid uploading sensitive or confidential material.

11. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Scan & Explain relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising delivery and measurement
  • Cloudflare Workers — Secure API routing and backend processing
  • Third-Party AI Providers (e.g., Google AI) — Image analysis and explanation generation
  • Google Play Services — App distribution and core Android functionality

12. YOUR CHOICES & CONTROLS

You can:

  • Choose which images to upload or cancel analysis at any time
  • Delete saved scan history directly inside the App
  • Reset or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device's Google Ads settings
  • Stop using or uninstall the App at any time

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates to the App, third-party services, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
